McMaster's First Aid program was constructed based on the principles of the WSIB Regulation 1101: First Aid Requirements handbook.

As per the terms of Regulation 1101, First Aid stations must be situated throughout the workplace at regular intervals so that injured persons can access treatment within 4 minutes. The stations must be staffed by an employee certified in Standard First Aid.

The First Aid Provider must complete an incident report in the case of any incident that requires first aid.

In the event of a serious injury, alert the local first aid provider and also call Campus Safety Services for emergency assistance (Ext. 88 from a McMaster phone, or 905-522-4135 from a cell phone).

Emergency First Response Team (EFRT)

In cases of serious injury or accident, McMaster University's Emergency First Response Team (EFRT) can provide advanced emergency first aid.

The EFRT team is staffed by trained, certified student volunteers. The team responds to incidents all across campus.

To access EFRT assistance, dial 905-522-4135 from your cell phone or 88 from a McMaster telephone and Campus Safety Services will dispatch a response member to your location.

Ambulance Assistance

To call for emergency ambulance assistance on the McMaster campus, dial 88 from a McMaster telephone (or 905-522-4135 from a cell phone). Campus Safety Services will dispatch McMaster's Emergency First Response Team and an ambulance to your location.

Dialing 911 from a cell phone is also possible, but 911 should not be dialed from a McMaster telephone (see Dialing 911).
