Job Hazard Assessment (JHA)

Download JHA form

As outlined in RMM 324: Job Hazard Analysis Program, the Job Hazard Assessment (JHA) form is to be completed by all new employees, including TAs and summer students.

The form should be completed with the aid of the employee's supervisor and should be approved before the intended start date.

Completed forms should be submitted to the department office, which will submit a second copy to UHS for filing purposes.
