In the event of an emergency, dialing 88 from any University telephone* will connect you to Campus Safety Services (formerly called Security Services), who will dispatch either the McMaster Emergency First Response Team (EFRT) or the necessary Emergency Service (i.e., an ambulance or police officer), if required.

* Except in the hospital, where the emergency number is 5555.

Note on the Former "Dial 88" Policy

Note: Previously, McMaster protocol was to dial 88 for on-campus emergencies, and 88 remains the number to dial from a McMaster telephone. 911 should only be dialed from a cell phone, not from a McMaster phone. McMaster is still working on the logistics of routing 911 calls to Campus Safety, and the implementation date is yet to be determined.

The reason Campus Safety Services handles all such calls is that they are able to guide emergency personnel directly to the location of the emergency. In many cases, this is a necessary intermediary point of contact to navigate around any campus events or closures that Emergency Services would not otherwise be aware of.

What if It Is Not an Emergency?

Emergency numbers should not be abused. If you need to contact Campus Safety for a non-emergency reason, dial extension 24281.

For example, just as you would not dial 911 for something as trivial as locking your keys in your car, it would be inappropriate to make an emergency call just because you locked your keys in your campus office or lab. The appropriate, non-emergency extension to call would be 24281 and a security representative will respond to help you back into your work area as soon as they are available.
