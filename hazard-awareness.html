Hazard Awareness

Hazard Awareness:
  • Chemical Hazards
  • Radiation Hazards
  • Laser Hazards
  • Electrical Hazards

In every workplace there exist hazards that could potentially lead to accidents or injuries. The CEDT and Engineering Physics are no exceptions. However, by identifying hazards, promoting awareness training, and implementing safety measures, the risk that a particular hazard could result in an incident is greatly mitigated.

Specific hazards commonly encountered in the CEDT and Engineering Physics can be loosely lumped into the following categories:

  • Chemical
  • Ionizing radiation (nuclear)
  • Ionizing radiation (electromagnetic)
  • Non-ionizing radiation (electromagnetic)
  • Laser
  • Electrical
