OHSA Section 28

The duties of a worker are explicitly defined in the OHSA. The worker is required to:

  • work in compliance with all prescribed workplace health and safety directives;
  • use protective equipment and devices as prescribed;
  • report to the supervisor any equipment defects;
  • report to the supervisor any workplace hazard.

According to the OHSA, Section 1(1), a Worker means "a person who performs work or supplies services for monetary compensation." This includes:

  • Laboratory Supervisors
  • Teaching Assistants (TAs)
  • Post-Doctoral Faculty
  • Research Engineers and Technical Staff
  • Research Associates/Fellows
  • Graduate Students (not technically employees but nonetheless Workers)
  • Summer Student Researchers

Workers have certain responsibilities, outlined in Section 28 of the OHSA. In practice, these responsibilities amount to taking prescribed training and completing documentation, working in accordance with written Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and reporting hazards to the appropriate supervisor so the situation can be corrected. Mandatory Documentation

Supervisors must complete the following documents:

  • Job Hazard Analysis
    To be completed with supervisor (i.e., Department Chair). Must be filed with the department office and UHS.
  • Contact Information and Lab Assessment form
    To be completed and posted on the door of all lab facilities.
  • Standard Operating Procedures
    SOPs must be completed for any procedure or process which:
    1. Could cause a critical injury.
    2. Could cause occupational illness.
    3. Could cause environmental impairment.
    4. Could cause damage to University property.
    It is recommended that you store a copy of the SOP both in the lab and in the office or online via a shared platform on Microsoft Teams.
  • Safety Data Sheet inventory
    Log all chemicals present in the lab with all relevant information including the approximate date purchased, purchaser, and volume. Keep a binder of SDS forms for each chemical. It is permitted though not recommended to upload these sheets onto Microsoft Teams or some other shared online platform.
  • Inspections of Facilities and Work Areas
    This requirement was introduced into RMM 302: Safety Audits and Inspections.

Training Students, Employees, Volunteers, and Visitors

Supervisors are responsible for ensuring anyone under their supervision completes the following documentation and mandatory training.

Note for visitors: Short-term visitors may not be able to complete certain mandatory training due to their time restrictions. In this circumstance, supervisors retain all responsibility for visitor safety. Site-specific training is required and work should be limited.
  • Job Hazard Analysis
  • Hazard Orientation Safety Report form
    To be completed when a student starts their degree or summer project and to be updated as needed if there are any significant changes to the project.
  • Mandatory Training
    Including any additional training required for their research/work area.
