Job Hazard Assessment

McMaster requires all employees to complete a safety form called the Job Hazard Assessment (JHA).
The JHA is intended for record-keeping purposes and reflects the identified mandatory training.

We ask that all employees arrange with their supervisor to complete a JHA within two weeks of their job appointment.

All persons working in CEDT or Engineering Physics facilities are required to read and abide by the following terms:

  1. Don't work in a lab or office unless the supervisor has given you permission.
  2. Don't work in any lab unless the lab supervisor has provided training in the safety procedures specific to the lab.
  3. Only use equipment if you've been trained in the safe operation of the equipment by the supervisor.
  4. You need to complete all Mandatory Training in a timely fashion.
  5. You need to familiarize yourself with the Faculty of Engineering and Faculty of Science Lab Safety Handbook.
