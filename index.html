This site has been developed as a comprehensive Health and Safety resource for faculty, staff, and students working in the Centre for Emerging Device Technologies (CEDT) and Engineering Physics (Eng Phys; EP).

Though two distinct organizations, overlap between the CEDT and Engineering Physics has fostered the establishment of a joint Health and Safety Committee. The motivation of the Committee in creating this site is to assist the CEDT and the Engineering Physics Department in meeting their responsibilities for matters of workplace health and safety as defined in McMaster University's Workplace and Environmental Health and Safety Policy.

Our internal health and safety programs are intended to implement the programs and practices laid out in McMaster University's Risk Management Manual (RMM), distributed by the University Health and Safety (UHS) department, and the Lab Safety Handbook (LSH), distributed by the Faculty of Engineering and the Faculty of Science. The RMM lays out an encompassing, university-wide safety policy program, and the LSH documents discuss safe laboratory practices.

This website aims to amalgamate and summarize the contents of the RMM and LSH most relevant to those working in the CEDT and Engineering Physics. It is hoped that the end result will be greater awareness of Health and Safety matters and, ultimately, a safer and more efficient workplace for our people.

CEDT / Engineering Physics Health and Safety Program

In recent years, the CEDT and the Engineering Physics Department have grown significantly.

To enhance the smooth operation of our facilities, the Health and Safety Committee has proposed new measures that will help ensure that our employees and supervisors are fully aware of their responsibilities and rights in their workplace and in conducting their research. These measures include:

  • the development of this website as a comprehensive means of addressing Health and Safety issues specific to the CEDT and Eng Phys;
  • the establishment of a CEDT / Eng Phys Safe Workplace Policy, with which all CEDT and Eng Phys employees and students are required to comply;
  • improved enforcement of basic laboratory safety practices.

For details, please see the Safety Program section of this site.

Have a Question? Ask Your Supervisor

If anything presented in this site leaves you uncertain, please feel free to discuss your concern with your supervisor or a representative of the H&S Committee.

You have the right to know about potential safety hazards in your work area; you have the right to bring safety concerns to your supervisor's attention and have those concerns addressed; and you have the right to refuse work that you believe is unsafe to your health.

Don't hesitate to ask questions.
